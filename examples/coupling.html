Two-way coupling — rigid bodies are moving boundaries of the MLS-MPM grid, and every grid node hands the momentum it loses at a boundary back to the body. Floating, sinking, drag and splashes are not scripted: bodies lighter than water float at Archimedes' waterline (to about a grid cell), and cubes of middling density roll onto an edge, as real ones do — upright they are unstable. alt/⌘-click drops a crate.
